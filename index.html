Our Story

A Taste of Thailand in Paris

Maison Thai brings the vibrant street-food culture and refined royal recipes of Thailand together in one warm, welcoming dining room. Every dish is prepared to order, balancing the classic Thai notes of sweet, sour, salty, and spicy.

  • Vegetarian & vegan options available
  • Spice level adjusted to your taste
  • Gluten-free options — ask our team
  • Ideal for lunch, dinner & private groups
